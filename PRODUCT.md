## Platform

## Users

Inferred from code: students preparing for campus placements, training and placement officers coordinating cohort readiness, and recruiters matching job descriptions to opted-in students.

## Product Purpose

Campus Career Copilot is an institutional placement and skill-readiness platform. It helps students analyze resumes, identify skill gaps, work through AI-generated learning journeys, compare cohort progress, and connect recruiter job requirements to qualified campus candidates.

## Positioning

Inferred from code: the product combines resume extraction, gap diagnosis, personalized journey generation, peer benchmarking, TPO cohort analytics, push interventions, and recruiter matching in one campus placement workflow.

## Operating Context

Students use the portal while preparing for campus drives, uploading PDF resumes, reviewing readiness scores, and completing skill milestones. TPO users monitor cohort diagnostics, seed demo data, and send targeted workshop notifications. Recruiters post job descriptions and review ranked candidate matches by skill overlap.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The app is a Next.js web product backed by Firebase authentication, Supabase data, Groq resume analysis, Firebase push notifications, Recharts analytics, and Leaflet campus mapping. Authenticated dashboards are role-routed for student, TPO, and recruiter users. Existing product terminology includes placement readiness, skill journeys, peer progress, cohort analytics, job descriptions, and competency radar.

## Product Principles

Make placement readiness visible and actionable.
Keep role-specific workflows direct and measurable.
Turn diagnosis into next steps, not just reports.
Preserve trust by separating real institutional data from illustrative/demo states.
Keep recruiter and TPO tools operational rather than decorative.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Inferred requirement: the interface should be keyboard-accessible, responsive, readable on projector/laptop/mobile contexts, and usable by students, institutional staff, and recruiters with clear role-specific navigation.
